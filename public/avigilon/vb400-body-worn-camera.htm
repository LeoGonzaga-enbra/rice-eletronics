VB400 Body-Worn Camera

Motorola Solutions’ VB400 body-worn camera for security staff and teams delivers live, first-person perspective video and audio of incidents. Built to withstand the rigors of the job, this rugged body-worn camera can cover an entire shift with its 12-hour battery life and provides greater situational awareness with its Avigilon Unity Video integration.

Get the full context of an event

Security teams in the control room need to know what’s happening on the ground so they can respond more effectively. Gain more context of an incident by adding a first-person perspective. Enabling the wearer to press record on the VB400 delivers a live video feed to the control room that can be displayed alongside fixed security camera footage, allowing for faster incident response and better protection.

Always ready to record

Never miss important interactions with the VB400’s ability to capture a continuous loop of video and audio before the moment the camera starts recording. Document critical events from multiple vantage points with peer-assisted recording. When your peers’ VB400s are activated within 10m, your camera also starts recording. Shift-long recording means better protection for lone-workers or security professionals working in hostile environments.

Extremely Easy to Use

Achieve transparency, protect people and prove professionalism with the VB400. From assigning a body-worn camera and recording footage to offloading video and creating incidents and cases, the VB400 and its associated VideoManager software are simple to use, easy to integrate and designed for everyday use. Video is secured on the VB400 to protect the integrity of your footage and support data protection compliance.
