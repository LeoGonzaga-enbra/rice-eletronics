Avigilon Technology to Protect Your Banks

Avigilon’s products and technology allow you to deploy a bank security system that can help you detect potential threats, respond quickly, and share information faster while complying with strict regulations.

Unified Video and Access Control
Easily verify the identity of a person attempting to access a bank using video and access control credentials, with the ability to unlock doors from the camera view.

Greater Image Detail When It Matters
Avigilon’s bank security cameras are built with exceptional image detail, Adaptive IR technology, thermal imaging, and more ‒ so you can detect faster and provide stronger visual evidence when an event occurs.
