Advanced Communication, Navigation & Monitoring Solutions for Marine, Offshore & Industrial Operations

From offshore rigs to global shipping fleets and industrial facilities, Rice Electronics delivers fully integrated systems that keep operations connected, visible, and under control.

We design, supply, and support mission-critical solutions—including marine communications, navigation systems, PAGA and alarm systems, explosion-proof intercoms, and topside and subsea video—engineered for performance in the world’s toughest environments.

With ISO 9001:2015-certified operations and a global presence spanning the U.S., Latin America, the Middle East, and beyond, our team provides 24/7 support, international logistics, and on-site installation crews in key regions including Singapore and Rotterdam.

Wherever your operation runs, Rice Electronics delivers the expertise, equipment, and support to keep it moving safely and efficiently.
