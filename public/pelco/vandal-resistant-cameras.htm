Vandal-proof security cameras

Our tamper-proof security cameras have vandal-resistant housing to prevent physical destruction and ensure uninterrupted monitoring.

Key benefits of vandal-resistant cameras

With Pelco’s vandal-proof cameras, you can manage safety and security at sites where there may be a risk of someone attempting to tamper or destroy indoor and outdoor security cameras.

Vandal-proof cameras

Video security you can rely on

Pelco’s vandal-proof cameras are designed to be rugged and tough, so they will continue working if subjected to direct blows or attempts to open the vandal-proof camera housing.

  • IK10 cameras
  • Weather resistant
  • Detect tampering

Enhanced coverage

Complete awareness

For high-risk areas, reliable video can save lives. Jails, schools and stores benefit from vandal-proof security cameras that are designed to withstand tampering so you can rely on 24⁄7 coverage.

  • Total coverage
  • Reduce crime
  • Added analytics

Improved operations

Save time and money

Pelco’s indoor and outdoor vandal-proof security cameras can enhance your security operations. Our vandal dome cameras can also help your organization reduce spend and wear and tear.

  • Open-platform vandal dome cameras
  • Easy installation and maintenance
  • Keep storage costs down
