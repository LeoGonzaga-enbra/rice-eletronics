Low-light security cameras

Pelco’s low-light security cameras help you observe movements in challenging lighting, whether dim indoor and low light or bright outdoor lighting.

Low-light IP cameras

Protect people and safeguard properties day and night with advanced low-light IP surveillance cameras from Pelco.

Optimal visibility

Clear video in any lighting

Advanced technology in our low-light dome cameras, bullet cameras, box cameras and PTZ cameras allows you to get clearer details and see more, even in extreme low-light.

  • See in near-total darkness
  • Expansive coverage at all times
  • Hone in on details, even in darkness

Extreme durability

Protect high-risk areas

Commonly used to monitor high-risk areas like schools and municipal buildings, our durable low-light video surveillance cameras can withstand blows and resist vandalism attempts.

  • Monitor without interruptions
  • Instant response to potential incidents
  • Observe activities indoors and outdoors

Thorough monitoring

Facilitate faster investigations

Take the guesswork out of investigations with the ultra low-light security cameras with SureVision technology that delivers high-quality images in the most difficult lighting conditions.

  • Optimized exposure for clear video
  • See through obscured surfaces
  • Never miss a detail
